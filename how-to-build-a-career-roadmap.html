How do I build a career roadmap in college?

Start with where you are and where you want to end up — the rest follows. Career Coach's free roadmap builder takes your year, major, interests, and target career, and in about two minutes turns them into a step-by-step plan: the clubs, classes, skills, certifications, and internships that get you there, mapped onto Tulane's own programs and recruiting timelines instead of generic advice.

Build my roadmap now See a worked example

Three steps, same as every tool on Career Coach

  1. 1

    Tell us about you

    Your year, major, interests, and where you want to end up — even "not sure yet" is a fine answer. It takes about two minutes.

  2. 2

    Get your roadmap

    A year-by-year timeline with milestones: clubs, classes, skills to build in order, certifications worth it, a networking plan, and internships to target, and when.

  3. 3

    Prep, apply, repeat

    Use the roadmap alongside the resume tools, job search, and the AI Career Coach whenever a question comes up.

What's actually in a roadmap

Not a generic checklist — a plan built around your answers and Tulane's own career ecosystem.

  • 🗺️A year-by-year timeline with milestones
  • 📅Recruiting timelines and key dates
  • 🎯Suggested career paths
  • 🤝Clubs and student organizations
  • 📚Classes and electives to consider
  • 🛠️Skills to build, in order
  • 💼Internships to target, and when

Your answers also save to your profile, so the AI Career Coach and the Career Toolkit's job search and resume tools know them too — you only enter them once.
