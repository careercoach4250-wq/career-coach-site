Resume

Results

Run the instant check for a match score, strengths, and quick fixes. AI feedback goes deeper and rewrites your weakest bullets.

One page, clean format

For students, one page almost always wins. Stick to one font, consistent dates, and clear section headings.

Action + what + result

Start every bullet with a strong verb and end with an outcome, ideally a number: "Grew club membership 40% by…"

Tailor it every time

Mirror the key skills in each job description, as long as they're true of you. Recruiters skim for them.

Order matters

Education, Experience, Leadership & Activities, then Skills. Your strongest, most relevant items go first.

Case Prep

Case interviews are standard in consulting and common in strategy, product, and some finance roles. Learn the structure, then practice a live case with an AI interviewer.

  1. ClarifyRestate the problem, confirm the goal, and ask 1–2 sharp questions.
  2. StructureLay out a clear, tailored framework before diving in.
  3. AnalyzeWork the data, do the math out loud, and say what it means.
  4. RecommendAnswer first, then the reasons, risks, and next steps.

Common case types

Case math, fast

Round to friendly numbers, keep track of zeros with "thousands / millions" labels, and sanity-check every answer against reality before you share it.

Questions

    Use STAR for behavioral answers
