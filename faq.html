Frequently asked questions

What Career Coach is, and isn't

The short, plain-language answers to what people ask before they try us. Every answer here is true as of November 2026.

What is Career Coach?

Career Coach is a free, AI-powered career platform built by seven Tulane University students. It helps you explore career paths, build a personalized roadmap, prepare your resume and interviews, and find jobs and internships, with an AI Career Coach you can ask anything, any time.

Who is Career Coach for?

Tulane University undergraduates and recent graduates, in any year or major, whether you have a clear goal or no idea yet. Access is limited to @tulane.edu email addresses, and the product is built around Tulane's roughly 8,500 undergraduates (source: Tulane Office of Undergraduate Admission).

What does Career Coach cost?

Nothing right now: $0, no card required (source: our pricing page). A paid tier may come later for deeper 1:1 coaching, but nothing is priced yet, and nobody will be charged without agreeing to a price first.

Where is Career Coach based?

Career Coach is built and run by seven Tulane University students in New Orleans, Louisiana. It's a website, not a physical office, so Tulane students can use it from anywhere.

How do I get started?

Use the roadmap builder or ask the AI Career Coach directly, no account needed. It takes about two minutes, and your answers are saved in your own browser. If you'd rather have a real person build your roadmap or review your resume, submit the Get Started form instead.

Do I need to create an account?

No. There's no sign-in system: your profile, roadmap, and chat history are saved locally in your own browser, not on our servers. Submitting the Get Started form does send your details to the team by email so a person can follow up.

How long does it take to get help?

A self-serve roadmap takes about two minutes to build, and the instant resume check returns results in seconds (timed by our own team). Requests that go to a real person, like a human-built roadmap or resume review, don't have a set turnaround time yet, though a mock interview can be booked instantly for any open calendar slot.

Is my information private?

The instant resume check runs entirely in your browser and is never uploaded anywhere. AI feedback sends your resume text to our AI provider to generate a response and isn't stored afterward, and anything you submit through a form goes only to the Career Coach team by email. See our Privacy Policy for the full picture.

How is Career Coach different from my university's career center?

It's not a replacement, it's built on top of it. Career Coach reads Tulane's own published resources, like the Freeman Career Management Center's recruiting timeline, into a personalized digital roadmap you can check any time, while resume reviews and mock interviews are still done by a real person.

How is Career Coach different from LinkedIn or a general AI chatbot?

Unlike a general AI chatbot, Career Coach's AI Career Coach already knows your major, year, and goals, and grounds its advice in facts specific to Tulane rather than generic answers. Unlike LinkedIn, there's no public profile to maintain, no account, and no cost.
