Why we exist.

Every student eventually asks "what should I do with my career?" Most figure it out through a patchwork of career-center visits, LinkedIn scrolling, and whoever's advice happens to be nearby. We think one of the biggest decisions of your twenties deserves better than that.

The gap we saw

Career planning isn't short on tools — it's short on a throughline connecting them. A resume template here, a job board there, a career-center appointment squeezed between classes. Nothing carries a student from "I don't know what I want" all the way to "I got the job."

That gap is growing, not shrinking: the career education and counseling services market is on track to grow from $2.84B in 2025 to $3.05B in 2026 — a 7.1% CAGR, outpacing general higher-ed market growth as job-market competition increases. (Source: The Business Research Company, Career Education Counseling Market Report)

What we believe

Belief 1

One roadmap beats a dozen tabs

A student needs one clear next step, not a dozen disconnected tools competing for attention.

Belief 2

Honest feedback over hype

Real strengths and real gaps, not empty encouragement — and no guarantees we can't back up.

Belief 3

Built for students, not corporations

Every decision starts from what actually helps the student in front of us, not what looks good on a sales deck.
