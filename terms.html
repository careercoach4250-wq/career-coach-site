Terms of Use

The ground rules for using Career Coach, in plain language.

Draft: these terms haven't been reviewed by a lawyer yet and will be finalized before Career Coach officially launches. Last updated September 24, 2026.

What Career Coach is

Career Coach offers career guidance, roadmaps, recruiting prep tools, job listings, and an AI career coach for students and recent graduates. It's currently free to use while we build it.

Guidance, not guarantees

Our tools and coaches give you our best guidance, but we can't guarantee any outcome, including interviews, job offers, or admissions. Recruiting timelines, clubs, courses, and requirements change, so confirm important details with your school and employers.

About the AI

The AI Career Coach and other AI tools can make mistakes or give incomplete answers. Treat what they say as a starting point, not professional, legal, financial, or medical advice. Review anything the AI writes for you, like a cover letter, before you use it, and make sure it's accurate about you.

Job listings

Job listings come from public, community-maintained sources. We don't screen each posting and aren't affiliated with the employers. Verify every listing on the employer's own site before you apply.

Using the site fairly

  • Don't misuse the site, try to break it, or overload our AI tools with automated requests.
  • Don't submit other people's personal information without permission.
  • Only use your own real information in forms and bookings.

Changes

We'll update these terms as Career Coach grows, and show the date of the latest change at the top of this page.
