When should I apply for internships?

Earlier than you think. Tulane's own Freeman Career Management Center publishes a recruiting timeline showing that for competitive tracks like investment banking, junior-year internship applications can open as early as January of sophomore year — more than a year before the internship starts. Consulting cycles usually open later, so you can run both in parallel without one crowding out the other. Career Coach turns Tulane's own recruiting calendar into a free, personalized roadmap, so you see which doors are opening months before everyone else does.

A real Tulane recruiting timeline, phase by phase

This is the shape of the timeline our career-pathing team builds out in full, with milestones, inside your roadmap. Shown here for a finance & investment-banking path — other paths (consulting, public health, law, and more) run on their own timelines.

Sophomore Fall

Apply to competitive finance clubs the moment applications open, and book a first coaching session at the career center — don't wait for "recruiting season."

Sophomore Spring

Sophomore/early-ID bank programs open. Freeman's own timeline flags junior-internship applications opening as early as January — this is the real cycle, not a formality.

Sophomore Summer → Junior Fall

Convert two years of groundwork into interviews. This is peak season: expect first rounds to run through the whole semester.

Junior Summer

The internship itself is a ten-week interview for the return offer.

Why timing catches students off guard

?

"Junior year" doesn't mean junior year

The applications that lead to a junior-summer internship often open during sophomore year, not junior year — by the time most students start looking, some windows have already opened.

?

Different fields, different clocks

Investment banking tends to move earliest; consulting typically opens later. Running both in parallel is possible once you know which clock is which.

?

Nobody hands you the calendar

Career centers publish these timelines, but they're easy to miss. Career Coach reads Tulane's own published timeline into your roadmap automatically.
