Start My Roadmap

One AI platform that carries you from “what should I do” to hired.

Five connected steps — nothing to stitch together yourself.

1

Discover

Identify career paths that fit each student's strengths. This is where the journey starts for students who don't yet know what they're aiming for.

2

Plan

Build a personalized roadmap toward that path — a clear sequence of what to do next, instead of guessing.

3

Prepare

Get AI resume feedback and practice mock interviews, so students walk into the real thing ready.

4

Connect

Find alumni, classes, clubs, and campus resources relevant to the path a student is on.

5

Apply

Match to relevant, open jobs — the last step of the journey, not a separate job board students have to figure out on their own.
