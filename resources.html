Why roadmap-first

We looked at how other career platforms across higher ed handle this. The ones that work best give students one clear next step instead of a dozen competing features to figure out on their own — so that's what Career Coach leads with: build your roadmap first, and let it point you toward resume feedback, interview prep, and connections as you need them.

Get plugged in at Tulane

Career Navigator's roadmaps are built on real research into Tulane's own career ecosystem, not generic advice. Here's a preview of what that includes.

Career services

Freeman Career Management Center

Tulane's own career center publishes its own recruiting timeline — for competitive tracks like investment banking, junior-year internship applications can open as early as January of sophomore spring.

Student orgs

Signature clubs, verified active

Green Bull Investment Banking Group, the Darwin Fenner Fund, and Wall Street Krewe are among the real, currently-active student organizations tied to finance and consulting recruiting at Tulane.

Academics

What makes Tulane different

A standout real estate program, a 3+3 law pipeline, a top-10-ranked public health school, a public-service graduation requirement, and Albert Lepage Center entrepreneurship funding all shape what a Tulane-specific roadmap can lean on.

Beyond campus

New Orleans and beyond

The New Orleans regional employer landscape, plus Tulane's alumni network in New York, DC, Atlanta, and Houston, extend a roadmap past graduation and into wherever a student ends up.
