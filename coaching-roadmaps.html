What a roadmap looks like

Every roadmap starts from your class year and where you want to end up. Here's an illustrative example, not a live roadmap.

Illustrative — 1st & 2nd year

Explore & commit

Discover 2–3 career paths that fit your strengths, sit in on a related class or club, and start a roadmap toward one.

Illustrative — 3rd year

Build & connect

Turn the roadmap into a resume, get feedback on it, and connect with alumni or classes tied to your target path.

Illustrative — 4th year

Prepare & apply

Run mock interviews, tighten your resume, and match to open roles in your target path.

Resume review, honestly explained

Paste your resume — and a job description, if you have one — and get back three things:

1

A match score

How well your resume overlaps with the role you're targeting.

2

Detected strengths

Bullets that already show quantified impact — the kind that stand out.

3

Improvement suggestions

Bullets missing a number or an action verb, and what's missing from the resume overall.
