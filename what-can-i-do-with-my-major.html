What can I do with my major?

Most majors open the door to far more careers than students realize — the paths just aren't obvious until someone points them out. Career Coach is a free platform built by seven Tulane students: tell our AI Career Coach your major, year, and interests, and it maps the specific career paths open to you, then turns the one you pick into a step-by-step roadmap built on Tulane's own clubs, programs, and recruiting timelines — not generic advice.

Your major is a starting point, not a box

A few examples of how one field of study at Tulane branches into very different careers. Your own mix of classes, interests, and goals will branch differently — this is what the AI Career Coach is for.

?

Finance or Economics

Branches into investment banking, consulting, corporate finance, or public policy — each with its own recruiting timeline and target clubs at Tulane. See the recruiting timeline for how early some of these move.

?

Pre-law or Political Science

Tulane's own 3+3 law pipeline lets eligible students finish a bachelor's and start law school a year early — one option among several for a pre-law major.

?

Public health & sciences

Tulane's School of Public Health and Tropical Medicine is ranked in the nation's top 10 — a strength most students outside the field never hear about.

?

Undecided, or exploring

"Not sure yet" is a fine starting answer. The roadmap builder and AI Career Coach both work without a declared major or target role.
